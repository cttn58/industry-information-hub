DriftSense H4 Questions and Answers

Answers to common questions about setup, readings, placement, cleaning, storage, and routine operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

QWhat is DriftSense H4?

A

A compact indoor comfort monitor using a simple colour indicator.

QDoes it require an account?

A

No account is required for basic local operation.

QWhere should it be placed?

A

On a stable indoor surface away from sun, water, steam, and concentrated airflow.

QIs it a safety alarm?

A

No. It is not a smoke, gas, medical, or emergency alarm.

QHow is it cleaned?

A

Disconnect power and wipe the exterior with a dry soft cloth.

QHow should it be stored?

A

Store dry, disconnected, and protected from dust.

QWhat operating environment is recommended?

A

DriftSense H4 is intended for operation between 5°C and 35°C and between 20% and 80% relative humidity.
