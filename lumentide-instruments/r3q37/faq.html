Frequently Asked Questions

Q What is DriftSense H4?

A

A compact indoor comfort monitor using a simple colour indicator.

Q Does it require an account?

A

No account is required for basic local operation.

Q Where should it be placed?

A

On a stable indoor surface away from sun, water, steam, and concentrated airflow.

Q Is it a safety alarm?

A

No. It is not a smoke, gas, medical, or emergency alarm.

Q How is it cleaned?

A

Disconnect power and wipe the exterior with a dry soft cloth.

Q How should it be stored?

A

Store dry, disconnected, and protected from dust.

Q What operating environment is recommended?

A

DriftSense H4 is intended for operation between 16°C and 26°C and between 25% and 60% relative humidity.
