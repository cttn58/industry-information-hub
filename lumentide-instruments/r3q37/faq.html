DriftSense H4 Questions and Answers

Answers to common questions about setup, readings, placement, cleaning, storage, and routine operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q — What is DriftSense H4?
A — A compact indoor comfort monitor using a simple colour indicator.
Q — Does it require an account?
A — No account is required for basic local operation.
Q — Where should it be placed?
A — On a stable indoor surface away from sun, water, steam, and concentrated airflow.
Q — Is it a safety alarm?
A — No. It is not a smoke, gas, medical, or emergency alarm.
Q — How is it cleaned?
A — Disconnect power and wipe the exterior with a dry soft cloth.
Q — How should it be stored?
A — Store dry, disconnected, and protected from dust.
