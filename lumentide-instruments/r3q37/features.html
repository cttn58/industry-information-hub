Components and Features

The design combines a compact enclosure, front indicator, ventilation slots, removable support stand, control button, and USB-C input.

Component Summary

Component Purpose
Ventilated enclosure Protects assemblies while allowing room air through openings.
Three-colour indicator Shows broad comfort bands.
Control button Changes modes and starts a local reset.
Detachable stand Supports stable tabletop placement.
