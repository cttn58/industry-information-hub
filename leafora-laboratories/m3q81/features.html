Ingredients & Features

Multivitamin X3 contains a carefully selected blend of vitamins and minerals designed to support everyday nutrition and wellness.

Key Features

  • Supports immune health
  • Helps maintain energy metabolism
  • Contributes to normal cognitive function
  • Supports normal nervous system function
  • Assists red blood cell formation
  • Helps maintain strong bones and teeth
  • Supports thyroid hormone production
  • Helps maintain overall wellness
  • Supports healthy vision and skin
  • Provides antioxidant protection against oxidative stress
