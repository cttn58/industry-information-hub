Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding Multivitamin X3, including ingredients, recommended usage, storage conditions, quality testing, and product information.

Q What is Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 is a daily multivitamin supplement developed by Leafora Laboratories. It is formulated to support overall health and wellness by providing essential vitamins and minerals that help maintain immune health, energy metabolism, cognitive function, and general well-being.
Q Who manufactures Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 is manufactured by Leafora Laboratories, a biotechnology, health, and wellness company specializing in vitamins, dietary supplements, sports nutrition products, skincare solutions, and functional foods.
Q Who is Multivitamin X3 intended for?
A Multivitamin X3 is designed for adults aged 18 years and above who wish to support their daily nutritional intake and overall wellness.
Q What are the key benefits of Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 helps support immune health, energy metabolism, and normal cognitive function. It also contributes to overall wellness by helping fill nutritional gaps that may exist in the daily diet.
Q What ingredients are included in Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 contains a blend of essential vitamins and minerals including Vitamin A, B-complex vitamins, Vitamins C, D3, E and K1, calcium, magnesium, zinc, selenium, copper, manganese, chromium, molybdenum, iodine and iron.
Q How should Multivitamin X3 be taken?
A The recommended usage is one capsule daily during or immediately after a meal, or as directed by a healthcare professional.
Q What type of packaging is used for Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 is packaged in an HDPE bottle containing 100 capsules, with an induction-sealed tamper-evident closure to help ensure product integrity.
Q How long is the shelf life of Multivitamin X3?
A Multivitamin X3 has a shelf life of 24 months from the date of manufacture when stored according to the recommended storage conditions.
Q How should Multivitamin X3 be stored?
A The product should be stored below 25°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. It should not be used if the bottle is damaged or if the tamper-evident seal is broken.
Q Has Multivitamin X3 undergone quality and safety testing?
A Yes. Multivitamin X3 undergoes comprehensive quality control testing, including identity testing, purity and contaminant testing, microbiological safety testing, potency verification, heavy metal analysis, and stability testing. All reported quality control assessments were passed.
