About Multivitamin X3

Multivitamin X3 is a comprehensive daily multivitamin supplement formulated to help adults maintain overall health and wellness. Developed by Leafora Laboratories, this scientifically balanced formula provides essential vitamins and minerals that support immune health, energy metabolism, cognitive function, and general well-being.

Each capsule contains a carefully selected blend of key nutrients, including Vitamins A, B-complex vitamins, Vitamins C and E, calcium, iodine, and iron. These nutrients work together to help fill nutritional gaps in the diet and support important bodily functions, including energy production, nervous system health, healthy vision, bone strength, and antioxidant protection.

Designed for adults aged 18 and above, Multivitamin X3 is suitable for inclusion in a daily wellness routine. The product is manufactured under strict quality standards and tested to ensure safety, purity, potency, and product consistency.

Product Claims

  • Supports immune health and energy metabolism
  • Contributes to normal cognitive function
  • Helps maintain overall wellness

Recommended Usage

One capsule daily, during or immediately after a meal, or as directed by a healthcare professional.

Shelf Life

24 months from the date of manufacture when stored according to the recommended storage conditions.

Storage Conditions

Store below 25°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not use if the bottle is damaged or if the tamper-evident seal is broken.
