Leafora Laboratory Perspectives

Scientific Illustration

Leafora Laboratories explores the use of scientific illustration to communicate complex biological concepts through clear diagrams, visual references, and educational displays. This work focuses on making scientific subjects easier to understand without presenting information about specific commercial products.

Research Terminology

The organization maintains internal terminology guidelines to encourage consistent language across general research documents. These guidelines address abbreviations, scientific naming conventions, writing clarity, and the presentation of technical concepts to readers with different levels of subject knowledge.

Laboratory Design

Workspace planning considers movement, lighting, documentation areas, shared equipment zones, and communication between teams. Layout reviews are intended to support orderly working practices and efficient coordination across research and administrative spaces.

Digital Documentation

General documentation practices include version control, document naming conventions, review histories, and organized digital archives. These practices help teams maintain clear records and locate internal materials during collaborative projects.

Interdisciplinary Dialogue

Periodic discussion sessions bring together participants from scientific, communication, design, and administrative backgrounds. The sessions examine how different professional perspectives can contribute to clearer planning, problem definition, and knowledge exchange.

Workplace Accessibility

Accessibility reviews consider readable documents, understandable navigation, adaptable communication formats, and practical access to shared workplace resources. The objective is to support an inclusive environment for people with different working and communication needs.
