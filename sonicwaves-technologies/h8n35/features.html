Components & Features

Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 combines advanced wireless audio technologies, premium components, Active Noise Cancellation, and up to 60 hours of battery life in a compact true wireless design.

Key Features

  • Bluetooth 5.4 wireless connectivity
  • Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)
  • Up to 60 hours of battery life with charging case
  • High-resolution audio support
  • Spatial audio tracking technology
  • Support for LDAC and aptX audio codecs
  • High sweat and water resistance
  • Built-in microphones for voice calls
  • Fast charging via USB-C connection
  • Touch controls for playback and voice assistants
  • Ergonomic lightweight design
  • Compact charging case

Company Information

People, Careers and Community

Learn more about career development, employee wellbeing, and community engagement at SonicWave Technologies, including the programmes and initiatives that support our people and the communities where we operate.

01

Product Development Careers

Acoustic engineers, firmware developers, industrial designers, procurement teams, packaging employees, and quality professionals can contribute different skills to product-development and improvement projects. Collaborative work may include user requirements, transducer selection, antenna performance, battery integration, touch controls, mechanical fit, supplier qualification, verification testing, packaging protection, and post-launch review, giving employees multiple pathways to develop specialist or project-lead responsibilities.

02

Audio and Wellbeing Education

Voluntary employee sessions may explain safe listening levels, hearing conservation, audio-codec basics, workplace wellbeing, and the distinction between general guidance and personal medical advice. Educational materials can also cover listening duration, suitable volume settings, device hygiene, ear-tip fit, communication accessibility, fatigue awareness, and when users or employees should seek advice from a qualified hearing or healthcare professional.

03

Supplier Responsibility Engagement

Corporate responsibility work may involve communicating expectations on component documentation, workplace standards, traceability, transport practices, battery handling, and responsible sourcing with suppliers. Reviews can examine material declarations, conflict-mineral reporting, restricted substances, quality records, packaging methods, corrective actions, labour expectations, environmental controls, and continuity planning while recognising that supplier requirements must be clear, proportionate, documented, and auditable.

04

Skills Recognition

Competency records and development reviews can recognise progress in technical knowledge, communication, documentation, teamwork, and operational problem-solving. Evidence may include completed training, supervised tasks, validated test results, improvement proposals, mentoring contributions, audit participation, and successful cross-functional assignments, enabling managers and employees to identify development priorities and plan fair progression toward specialist, supervisory, or project roles.

05

Research Communication

Employees may receive guidance on communicating research, product information, and regulatory documentation clearly while avoiding unsupported performance or health claims. Good practice includes identifying evidence sources, separating test results from interpretation, using consistent terminology and units, documenting limitations, reviewing public statements, protecting confidential information, and adapting explanations for engineers, regulators, customer-service teams, retailers, and general audiences.
