Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1, including connectivity, battery life, product features, storage recommendations, and quality testing.

Q What is Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1?
A Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 is a premium true wireless audio solution designed to deliver high-quality sound, reliable wireless connectivity, and all-day comfort for everyday listening.
Q Who manufactures Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1?
A Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 is manufactured by SonicWave Technologies, a consumer electronics company specializing in audio products and wireless communication devices.
Q Who is Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 intended for?
A Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 is suitable for users of all ages who require high-quality wireless audio for music, calls, travel, sports, commuting, and everyday activities.
Q What are the key features of Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1?
A Key features include Bluetooth 5.4 connectivity, Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), high-resolution audio support, spatial audio tracking, up to 60 hours of battery life, sweat and water resistance, and support for LDAC and aptX audio codecs.
Q What technologies are included in Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1?
A The earphones include Bluetooth 5.4 wireless technology, Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), MEMS microphones, spatial audio tracking, dynamic speaker drivers, capacitive touch controls, and advanced audio codec support.
Q How should Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 be used?
A The earphones can be used for music playback, voice calls, video streaming, fitness activities, commuting, travel, and daily entertainment. Users may control playback and calls using the built-in touch controls.
Q What is included in the package?
A The package includes the wireless earphones, charging case, USB-C charging cable, ear tips in three sizes, quick start guide, user manual, and warranty card.
Q How long is the shelf life of Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1?
A Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 has a recommended service life of three years from the manufacturing date under normal usage conditions.
Q How should Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 be stored?
A The earphones should be stored at a stable room temperature. Users should ensure the earbuds are completely dry and free of sweat or rain before placing them into the charging case to help prevent electrical shorts and corrosion.
Q Has Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 undergone quality and safety testing?
A Yes. Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 undergoes extensive quality-control testing, including electronic component verification, battery safety inspection, PCB functionality testing, Bluetooth module verification, connectivity testing, audio performance evaluation, ANC verification, battery and charging evaluation, water and sweat resistance testing, durability testing, EMC testing, and final visual inspection. All reported tests were passed.
