Product Specifications

Physical Description

  • Compact true wireless in-ear earphones
  • Ergonomic and lightweight design
  • Available in black finish
  • Smooth matte surface with premium appearance
  • Soft silicone ear tips in three sizes (S, M, L)
  • Portable charging case with LED battery status indicator
  • No visible scratches, cracks, deformation, or cosmetic defects
  • Normal operation of touch controls and charging contacts

Packaging

  • Wireless earphones
  • Charging case
  • Ear tips in 3 sizes
  • USB-C charging cable
  • Quick start guide
  • User manual
  • Warranty card

Warnings

  • Listening to high volumes or using earbuds for many hours straight can cause permanent hearing loss or ringing in the ears.
  • Store at a stable room temperature.
  • Ensure earbuds are completely dry and free of sweat or rain before placing them into the case.
  • Prevent electrical shorts and corrosion by keeping charging contacts dry.
