Designed for television audio

Expanded home audio with wired and wireless connectivity

SonicRidge SoundBar S24 is a 3.1-channel television soundbar with a total output power of 150 watts.

The 980-millimetre soundbar provides HDMI ARC, optical audio and Bluetooth 5.3. A wireless subwoofer supports lower-frequency sound.

Movie, Music and Voice sound modes adjust the sound profile for different television programmes and audio sources.

Connection guidance

How to connect SoundBar S24

Follow these steps when connecting the soundbar.

  1. 1

    Position the soundbar

    Place it centrally beneath the television.

  2. 2

    Position the subwoofer

    Place the wireless subwoofer nearby with ventilation space.

  3. 3

    Select the input

    Choose HDMI ARC, optical or Bluetooth.

  4. 4

    Select a sound mode

    Choose Movie, Music or Voice mode.

i

Recommended placement

Keep the soundbar grille and subwoofer ventilation openings unobstructed.

Important information

Safety and Care

Follow these precautions when using SoundBar S24.

Installation

Use stable surfaces

  • Keep the soundbar and subwoofer stable.
  • Do not block ventilation openings.
  • Keep both units away from water.
  • Use the supplied power adapters.
Operation

Use a suitable volume

  • Begin playback at a low level.
  • Avoid prolonged high-volume listening.
  • Keep cables away from walkways.
  • Disconnect during electrical storms.
Cleaning

Disconnect before cleaning

Clean both units using a soft, dry cloth.
