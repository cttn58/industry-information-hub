Designed for television audio

Compact sound with wired and wireless connectivity

SonicRidge SoundBar S24 is a 2.0-channel television soundbar with a total output power of 60 watts.

The 720-millimetre soundbar provides HDMI ARC and Bluetooth 4.2. It does not include a subwoofer or optical audio input.

Movie, Music and Voice sound modes adjust the sound profile for different television programmes and audio sources.

Important information

Safety and Care

Follow these precautions when using SoundBar S24.

Installation

Use a stable surface

  • Keep the soundbar stable.
  • Do not block ventilation openings.
  • Keep it away from water.
  • Use the supplied adapter.
Operation

Use a suitable volume

  • Begin playback at a low level.
  • Avoid prolonged high-volume listening.
  • Keep cables away from walkways.
  • Disconnect during electrical storms.
Cleaning

Disconnect before cleaning

Clean the soundbar using a soft, dry cloth.
