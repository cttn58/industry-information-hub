Portable airflow with evaporative cooling support

PolarMist CoolBreeze M30 is a portable evaporative air cooler with a 7-litre removable water tank.

The unit provides three fan speeds, a maximum airflow of 450 cubic metres per hour and a rated power of 65 watts.

The control panel and supplied remote control can be used to adjust fan speed, cooling mode, automatic horizontal oscillation and the 7.5-hour timer.

Operating guidance

How to use CoolBreeze M30

Follow these steps when filling and operating the air cooler.

  1. 1

    Position the cooler

    Place the unit upright on a level surface near an open door or window.

  2. 2

    Fill the tank

    Add clean water without exceeding the 7-litre maximum level.

  3. 3

    Select the settings

    Choose a fan speed, cooling mode, oscillation and timer setting.

  4. 4

    Drain before storage

    Empty and dry the tank before extended storage.

i

Recommended ventilation

Keep a door or window partly open when using evaporative cooling mode.

Important information

Safety and Maintenance

Follow these precautions when operating CoolBreeze M30.

Before operation

Inspect the cooler

  • Place the cooler on a level surface.
  • Do not operate it with a damaged cord or plug.
  • Do not fill above the 7-litre maximum water level.
  • Keep the air inlet and outlet clear.
During operation

Keep the unit upright

  • Do not move the unit while the tank is full.
  • Do not insert objects into the air outlet.
  • Keep the remote control dry.
  • Disconnect power before refilling the tank.
Maintenance

Clean the tank and filter

Disconnect the cooler, drain the water tank and clean the washable dust filter regularly.
