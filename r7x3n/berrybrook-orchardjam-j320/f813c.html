Made for everyday serving

Strawberry flavour in a practical resealable jar

BerryBrook OrchardJam J320 is a strawberry fruit preserve made with 50% strawberries and packed in a 320 gram glass jar.

The jam combines strawberries, sugar, lemon juice and fruit pectin to create a smooth, spreadable consistency. Each 100 gram serving contains 238 kilocalories and 46 grams of total sugar.

OrchardJam J320 can be served on bread, toast, pastries, yoghurt or porridge. After opening, the jar should be refrigerated with the lid closed and used within four weeks.

Serving guidance

How to serve OrchardJam J320

Follow these steps when opening, serving and storing the product.

  1. 1

    Check the seal

    Before opening, check that the jar and lid are undamaged and that the safety seal has not been broken.

  2. 2

    Use a clean utensil

    Use a clean, dry spoon or knife to remove the required quantity of jam from the jar.

  3. 3

    Serve as required

    Spread directly onto bread, toast or pastries, or add a measured portion to yoghurt, porridge or desserts.

  4. 4

    Refrigerate

    Close the lid securely, refrigerate the jar and use the remaining contents within four weeks.

i

Recommended storage

After opening, keep OrchardJam J320 refrigerated with the lid securely closed and consume the contents within four weeks.

Important information

Food Safety and Storage

Follow these precautions when opening, serving and storing OrchardJam J320.

Before opening

Check the packaging

  • Do not use the product if the glass jar is cracked.
  • Do not use the product if the lid is loose or damaged.
  • Check the best-before date before serving.
  • Store the unopened jar away from heat and direct sunlight.
After opening

Keep the product refrigerated

  • Close the lid securely after each use.
  • Use only clean, dry utensils.
  • Keep the opened jar refrigerated.
  • Use the contents within four weeks.
