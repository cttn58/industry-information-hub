General Product Information

DailyLoaf B650 combines a 650 gram loaf size, 14 pre-cut slices, whole-wheat flour and a four-day freshness period in one packaged bread product.

After the package is opened, the required slices should be removed before excess air is pressed from the package and the opening is closed securely.

The resealed loaf should be kept in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight, heat, moisture and strong odours.

Bread and Storage Information

To use DailyLoaf B650, open the package, remove the required number of whole-wheat bread slices, press excess air from the package, close the opening securely and return the loaf to a cool and dry place.

The remaining loaf should be consumed within four days after opening, and the bread should not be used if visible mould, an unusual odour or another sign of spoilage is present.
