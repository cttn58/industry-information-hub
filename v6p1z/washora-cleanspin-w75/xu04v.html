Designed for everyday laundry

Flexible washing programs in a practical 7.5 kg appliance

Washora CleanSpin W75 is a front-loading washing machine designed to handle everyday household laundry with a maximum washing capacity of 7.5 kg.

The appliance provides 12 washing programs, a maximum spin speed of 1,200 rpm and a delay-start function that allows a selected cycle to begin later.

The delicate program is intended for suitable lightweight or fragile clothing and uses reduced mechanical action and a lower spin setting to help limit fabric stress.

Delicate clothing guidance

How to use the delicate program

Follow these steps to prepare, load and wash suitable delicate clothing in the CleanSpin W75.

  1. 1

    Check the labels

    Confirm that every garment care label permits machine washing and follow any stated temperature or spin restrictions.

  2. 2

    Prepare the garments

    Fasten zips, empty pockets, turn decorated garments inside out and place smaller delicate items in a suitable laundry bag.

  3. 3

    Select the settings

    Select the delicate program with cold water or 30°C and choose a spin speed of no more than 600 rpm when the care labels permit these settings.

  4. 4

    Remove the load promptly

    Remove the garments when the cycle finishes and dry each item according to its care label.

i

Recommended delicate settings

Keep the load below 2 kg, select cold water or 30°C according to the care labels and use a spin speed of no more than 600 rpm.

Important information

Laundry Care and Appliance Safety

Follow these precautions when preparing delicate garments and operating the CleanSpin W75.

Before washing

Check the laundry

  • Check every garment care label before selecting a washing program.
  • Remove items marked for hand washing or dry cleaning only.
  • Empty all pockets and remove loose objects from the garments.
  • Do not exceed the recommended 2 kg delicate-program load.
During operation

Use appropriate settings

  • Select the dedicated delicate program for suitable garments.
  • Use cold water or 30°C when permitted by the care labels.
  • Keep the spin speed at or below 600 rpm for delicate loads.
  • Use a detergent suitable for the garments and selected temperature.
Notice

Garment-specific instructions

The instructions printed on each garment care label take priority over the general program recommendations provided for the CleanSpin W75.
