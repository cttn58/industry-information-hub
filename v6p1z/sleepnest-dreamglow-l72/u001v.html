Adjustable light with integrated charging

SleepNest DreamGlow L72 is a bedside lamp with adjustable brightness for different room and activity requirements.

The lamp provides warm, neutral and cool colour temperatures and includes a USB charging connection for a compatible device.

Neutral light at approximately 60 to 70 percent brightness is recommended for typical bedside reading.
