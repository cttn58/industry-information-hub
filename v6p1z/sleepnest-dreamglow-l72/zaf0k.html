DreamGlow L72 Overview

SleepNest produces DreamGlow L72 as a bedside lamp with adjustable brightness, three colour temperatures and a USB charging connection for a compatible device.

The product name is DreamGlow, the model is L72, the brand and manufacturer are SleepNest, the product category is bedside lamp, the brightness is adjustable, the lamp provides warm, neutral and cool light and the additional function is USB charging.

General Product Information

DreamGlow L72 combines adjustable brightness, three colour temperatures and USB charging in one bedside lighting product.

Warm light provides softer evening illumination, neutral light supports typical reading and cool light provides a brighter-looking tone for focused tasks.

For typical bedside reading, neutral light should be selected at approximately 60 to 70 percent brightness.

Lamp and Reading Information

To use DreamGlow L72 for reading, place the lamp beside or slightly behind the reading position, select neutral light, set the brightness to approximately 60 to 70 percent and aim the light toward the page.

The light should not be directed into the eyes, and the USB charging connection should be used only with a compatible device and cable.
