WarmKettle K17 Overview

HeatHaven produces WarmKettle K17 as a 1.7 litre electric kettle with 2,200 watt heating power, selectable water temperatures and a keep-warm mode for maintaining the chosen setting.

The product name is WarmKettle, the model is K17, the brand and manufacturer are HeatHaven, the product category is electric kettle, the capacity is 1.7 litres, the heating power is 2,200 watts and the available controls include temperature selection and keep-warm mode.

General Product Information

WarmKettle K17 combines a 1.7 litre capacity, 2,200 watt heating, temperature selection and keep-warm operation in one water-heating appliance.

The recommended drink temperatures are 70°C for white tea, 80°C for green tea, 90°C for coffee and 100°C for black tea or boiling water.

Keep-warm mode can be enabled when the selected water temperature should be maintained for later use.

Kettle and Temperature Information

To use WarmKettle K17, fill the kettle without exceeding the 1.7 litre maximum, close the lid, identify the drink, select 70°C, 80°C, 90°C or 100°C and start the heating operation.

The kettle should be held by its handle when hot water is poured, and keep-warm mode should be used when more water may be required at the selected temperature.
