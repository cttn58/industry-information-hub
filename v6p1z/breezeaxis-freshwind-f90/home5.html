FreshWind F90 Overview

BreezeAxis produces the FreshWind F90 standing fan for household air circulation, and the product has five selectable speed settings, 90-degree oscillation for distributing airflow across a room, a timer for scheduled operation and remote control for adjusting the main settings from a distance.

The product name is FreshWind, the model is F90, the brand and manufacturer are BreezeAxis, the product category is standing fan, the fan provides five speeds, the oscillation range is 90 degrees and the available controls include a timer and remote control.

General Product Information

FreshWind F90 provides five airflow levels and 90-degree oscillation, while the timer allows the operating period to be scheduled and the remote control allows the main settings to be adjusted from a distance.

For typical bedroom use, Speed 1 or 2 is recommended for gentler airflow, oscillation should be enabled to distribute air through the room and a one-to-two-hour timer should be used when continuous overnight operation is unnecessary.

The five-speed figure refers to the number of selectable airflow levels, the 90-degree figure refers to the oscillation range, the timer refers to scheduled fan operation and the remote control provides access to the main settings from a distance.

Fan and Bedroom Settings

To use FreshWind F90, place the fan on a stable surface with a clear airflow path, choose one of the five speeds, enable 90-degree oscillation when wider air distribution is required, use the timer when the fan should switch off later and use the remote control to adjust the available settings from a distance.

For bedroom operation, position the fan so that airflow moves across the room rather than continuously toward the bed, select Speed 1 or 2, enable oscillation and set a timer of one to two hours when the fan does not need to operate throughout the night.
