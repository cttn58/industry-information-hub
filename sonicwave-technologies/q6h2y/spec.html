Product Specifications

Physical Description

  • Compact true wireless in-ear earphones designed for portability, daily convenience, and comfortable long-term use
  • Ergonomic and lightweight construction intended to reduce fatigue during extended listening sessions
  • Available in a premium black finish suitable for professional and casual environments
  • Smooth matte surface designed to provide a professional appearance while helping reduce visible fingerprints
  • Medical-grade silicone ear tips in three sizes (S, M, L) to improve comfort, fit, and passive noise isolation
  • Portable charging case equipped with LED battery indicators to help users monitor charging status and remaining power levels
  • Manufactured to be free from visible scratches, cracks, deformation, discoloration, and cosmetic imperfections
  • Touch-control surfaces and charging contacts verified to function correctly according to product specifications

Packaging

  • Wireless earphones with Bluetooth 5.4 wireless connectivity
  • Rechargeable charging case for storage and battery charging
  • Ear tips in three sizes (small, medium, and large) for customized fit
  • USB-C charging cable for convenient charging and maintenance
  • Quick start guide providing basic setup instructions
  • User manual containing operational and safety information
  • Warranty card containing warranty and support information

Warnings

  • Listening to audio at excessive volume levels or for extended periods may increase the risk of permanent hearing damage or ringing in the ears
  • Store the earphones and charging case at a stable room temperature away from liquids and excessive humidity
  • Ensure earbuds are completely dry and free from sweat, rain, or moisture before placing them in the charging case
  • Keep charging contacts clean and dry to reduce the risk of electrical shorts, corrosion, or charging interruptions
