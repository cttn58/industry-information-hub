Components & Features

Bluetooth 5.4 Wireless Earphones X1 combines advanced wireless audio technologies, premium hardware components, Active Noise Cancellation (ANC), high-resolution audio, spatial audio tracking, and long battery life in a compact true wireless design. The earphones are engineered to provide stable wireless connectivity, immersive sound reproduction, and reliable performance across a wide variety of listening environments. Designed for music playback, multimedia entertainment, hands-free communication, travel, fitness activities, commuting, and everyday use, the product delivers a balance of sound quality, user comfort, durability, and portability. Integration of Bluetooth 5.4 technology helps improve transmission stability, reduce latency, and increase power efficiency compared to earlier wireless communication standards.

Key Features

  • Bluetooth 5.4 wireless connectivity for stable and low-latency audio transmission
  • Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) technology to reduce surrounding environmental noise
  • Up to 60 hours of total battery life when used together with the charging case
  • High-resolution audio support for enhanced clarity and audio detail
  • Spatial audio tracking technology for immersive listening experiences
  • Support for premium wireless audio codecs including LDAC and aptX
  • Sweat-resistant and water-resistant construction suitable for sports and outdoor use
  • Built-in MEMS microphones for clear voice communication and call quality
  • USB-C fast charging support for convenient power replenishment
  • Touch-sensitive controls for playback, phone calls, and voice assistants
  • Ergonomic lightweight construction designed for long-term wearing comfort
  • Portable charging case providing both charging functionality and convenient storage
