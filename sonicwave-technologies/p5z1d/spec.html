  • Quick start guide
  • User manual
  • Warranty card

Warnings

  • Listening to high volumes or using earbuds for many hours straight can cause permanent hearing loss or ringing in the ears.
  • Store at a stable room temperature.
  • Ensure earbuds are completely dry and free of sweat or rain before placing them into the case.
  • Prevent electrical shorts and corrosion by keeping charging contacts dry.
