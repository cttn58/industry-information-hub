Wireless listening setup

Pair and configure the headphones for everyday listening

EchoVanta CallClear H12 headphones use Bluetooth 5.3 to connect to a compatible phone, tablet or computer. They provide up to 40 hours of battery life, active noise cancellation and a foldable design for compact storage.

Before pairing, charge the headphones sufficiently and place them close to the device. Turn on Bluetooth in the device settings, then press and hold the headphone power button until the pairing indicator begins flashing. This indicates that the headphones are ready to be discovered.

Open the device Bluetooth list and select “CallClear H12.” Accept the pairing request if one appears. Wait until the connected status is shown or the headphone indicator changes from its pairing pattern. Play a short audio sample to confirm that sound is being routed through the headphones.

After connection, adjust the device volume to a comfortable level and enable noise cancellation when reduced environmental sound is useful. Keep the headphones within normal Bluetooth range and remove major obstacles between the headphones and connected device to support a stable signal.

When the headphones are not being used, turn them off and fold the earcups inward using the intended hinges. Store them in a dry place without forcing the joints or placing heavy objects on the folded headphones.

Comprehensive guidance

How to pair and configure CallClear H12

Follow these steps to charge the headphones, enter pairing mode, establish a Bluetooth connection and configure audio and noise cancellation.

  1. 1

    Charge and prepare

    Charge CallClear H12 sufficiently before setup. Keep the headphones near the phone, tablet or computer and make sure no other device is actively trying to reconnect to them. Turn the source-device Bluetooth setting on and open its available-device list.

  2. 2

    Enter pairing mode

    With the headphones switched off or disconnected, press and hold the power button until the pairing indicator begins flashing. Release the button when the pairing pattern appears and leave the headphones powered on while the device searches.

  3. 3

    Connect and test

    Select “CallClear H12” from the available Bluetooth devices and accept the request if prompted. Wait for confirmation, select the headphones as the audio output and play a short sample at low volume to confirm a successful connection.

  4. 4

    Configure and use

    Set a comfortable listening volume and enable active noise cancellation when appropriate. Keep awareness of traffic, alarms and people nearby. After use, switch the headphones off, fold them through the intended hinges and store them safely.

i

Connection reminder

If CallClear H12 does not connect, switch Bluetooth off and on, remove the existing CallClear H12 entry from the device and repeat the pairing process with the headphones close to the source device.

Important information

Listening Safety and Storage

Follow these precautions when charging, configuring, using and storing EchoVanta CallClear H12.

Before pairing

Prepare the headphones and device

  • Charge the headphones before extended use.
  • Inspect the headphones and charging connection for damage.
  • Keep the headphones close to the device during pairing.
  • Turn on Bluetooth and open the available-device list.
  • Disconnect another device if it reconnects automatically.
During use

Use a comfortable volume

  • Begin playback at a low volume.
  • Avoid extended listening at uncomfortable levels.
  • Do not rely on noise cancellation for hearing protection.
  • Maintain awareness near traffic, alarms and warnings.
  • Stop using the headphones if discomfort occurs.
After use

Fold and store carefully

Switch the headphones off before storage. Fold the earcups using the intended hinges without twisting the headband, then keep the headphones in a clean, dry location away from moisture, excessive heat and heavy objects.

If the headphones will not be used for an extended period, recharge them periodically and check that the charging port and folding joints remain clean and undamaged.
