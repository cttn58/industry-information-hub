Questions and Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct answers about BrightPath S50 installation and use.

QWhat is BrightPath S50?
A

BrightPath S50 is a 500-lumen solar garden light with a motion sensor, IP65 rating and up to 10 hours of runtime.

QWhere should it be installed?
A

Install it in firm soil where the panel receives open daylight.

QHow should the sensor be aimed?
A

Aim the sensor toward the expected approach route.

QHow is the light tested?
A

Charge it during daylight and walk through the sensor area after dark.

QHow long does it operate?
A

Runtime is up to 10 hours, depending on charging and use.

QCan it be submerged?
A

No. The IP65-rated light must not be submerged.

QWhat if it does not activate?
A

Check the switch, clean and recharge the panel, then test the sensor again.
