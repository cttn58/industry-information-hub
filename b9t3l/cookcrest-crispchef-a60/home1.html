Frozen-fries preparation

Arrange, cook, shake and check the fries before serving

CrispChef A60 combines a six-litre basket, 1,700-watt heating system, eight cooking programs and adjustable temperature control for air-frying frozen fries and other suitable foods.

Place the air fryer on a stable, level and heat-resistant surface with sufficient clearance around its air inlet and outlet. Keep the appliance away from walls, curtains and other heat-sensitive or combustible materials, and make sure the basket is clean and dry.

Check the frozen-fries packaging for its recommended air-fryer temperature and cooking time. Use those instructions as the starting point because fry thickness, coating, quantity and brand can change the required settings. Preheat the air fryer if the product instructions or selected program recommends it.

Place the frozen fries in the basket without tightly packing or overfilling it. Leaving space between the fries allows heated air to circulate. Insert the basket fully, select the fries program or a suitable manual temperature and begin cooking.

At approximately the middle of the cooking period, pull out the basket using its handle and shake or turn the fries. Reinsert the basket promptly and continue cooking. Shaking redistributes the fries so different surfaces are exposed to the circulating air.

Check the fries near the end of the selected time. Continue cooking briefly if they are not evenly golden, crisp and thoroughly hot. Remove the basket carefully, avoid contact with hot surfaces and use non-metal utensils where necessary before serving.

Comprehensive guidance

How to cook frozen fries in CrispChef A60

Follow these steps to prepare the appliance, load the basket, select the cooking setting, shake the fries and check them before serving.

  1. 1

    Prepare the appliance

    Place CrispChef A60 on a stable, level and heat-resistant surface with open space around its vents. Check that the basket is clean and dry, then review the frozen-fries package instructions and preheat if recommended.

  2. 2

    Load and select

    Place an appropriate quantity of frozen fries in the basket without tightly packing them. Insert the basket fully and select the fries program or a manual time and temperature based on the package guidance.

  3. 3

    Shake during cooking

    At approximately the midpoint, remove the basket by its handle and shake or turn the fries carefully. Reinsert the basket and continue cooking so the redistributed fries receive more even exposure to the hot air.

  4. 4

    Check and serve

    Check the fries near the end of the selected time. Continue cooking briefly if needed, then confirm that they are thoroughly hot and evenly cooked. Remove them carefully using suitable utensils.

i

Cooking reminder

Cooking time varies with fry thickness, brand, coating, quantity and starting temperature. Use package guidance first, shake the basket midway and add time only in short intervals.

Important information

Cooking Safety and Cleaning

Follow these precautions when positioning, operating and cleaning CookCrest CrispChef A60.

Before cooking

Prepare the appliance safely

  • Use a stable, level and heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep the air inlet and outlet unobstructed.
  • Keep the appliance away from curtains and walls.
  • Check that the basket is clean and correctly inserted.
  • Connect the appliance to a suitable electrical outlet.
During cooking

Handle hot parts carefully

  • Use the basket handle and avoid hot surfaces.
  • Keep hands and face away from hot exhaust air.
  • Do not cover the appliance during operation.
  • Do not pour excessive oil into the basket.
  • Supervise the appliance while it is operating.
After cooking

Cool and clean the basket

Switch off and unplug the air fryer after use. Allow the appliance and basket to cool before cleaning. Remove food residue with a soft sponge and mild dishwashing liquid.

Do not immerse the main appliance in water. Avoid abrasive tools that may damage the basket coating, then dry the removable parts before reassembly and storage.
