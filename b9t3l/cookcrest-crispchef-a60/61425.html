Important information

Cooking Safety and Cleaning

Position, operate and clean the air fryer carefully.

Before cooking

Prepare safely

  • Use a stable, heat-resistant surface.
  • Keep the vents clear.
  • Check the basket before use.
During cooking

Handle carefully

  • Use the basket handle.
  • Avoid hot exhaust air.
  • Supervise the appliance.
After cooking

Cool and clean

Unplug and cool the fryer before cleaning, and do not immerse the main unit.

Questions and Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct answers about CrispChef A60 and frozen-fries cooking.

QWhat is CrispChef A60?
A

CrispChef A60 is a 1,700-watt air fryer with a six-litre basket, eight programs and adjustable temperature.

QWhich setting should be used?
A

Use the fries program or a manual setting based on the package guidance.

QShould the basket be overcrowded?
A

No, leave room for heated air to circulate.

QWhen should the fries be shaken?
A

Shake them approximately halfway through cooking.

QWhen are the fries ready?
A

They should be thoroughly hot and cooked to the desired crispness.

QHow is the fryer cleaned?
A

Unplug and cool it before cleaning the removable basket.

QCan the main unit be immersed?
A

No, never immerse the main appliance in water.
