Important information

Filter Safety and Jug Care

Prepare, replace and maintain the filter system carefully.

Before replacement

Prepare the jug

  • Wash your hands and empty the jug.
  • Clean and rinse the components.
  • Use a compatible, undamaged filter.
During replacement

Install and flush

  • Seat the filter firmly.
  • Use cold potable water.
  • Discard initial water as directed.
After replacement

Reset and maintain

Reset the indicator and keep the jug clean during regular use.

Questions and Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find direct answers about FreshPour F30 filter replacement.

QWhat is FreshPour F30?
A

FreshPour F30 is a BPA-free three-litre water-filter jug with a replaceable cartridge and indicator.

QHow is the old filter removed?
A

Empty the jug and pull the used cartridge from the reservoir.

QHow is a new filter prepared?
A

Rinse or soak it according to its supplied instructions.

QHow is it installed?
A

Press the prepared filter firmly into the reservoir.

QIs flushing required?
A

Yes, discard the initial filtered water as directed.

QWhen is the indicator reset?
A

Reset it after the replacement filter is installed and flushed.

QWhat water should be used?
A

Use cold potable tap water and do not use hot water.
