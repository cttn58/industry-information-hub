Simple meal preparation

Vegetable soup prepared from one convenient can

QuickHarvest GardenSoup S400 is a canned vegetable soup with a tomato and vegetable base. The 400 gram can contains enough soup for two servings and is ready to heat without adding water or preparing additional ingredients.

GardenSoup S400 can be heated in a saucepan on a stovetop or in a suitable microwave-safe bowl. The soup should always be removed completely from the metal can before heating. The can itself must not be placed on a stovetop, in a microwave oven or in any other heating appliance.

For stovetop preparation, pour the soup into a saucepan and heat it over a medium setting. Stir regularly so the tomato and vegetable base heats evenly and does not remain against the bottom of the pan. Continue heating until the soup is thoroughly hot.

For microwave preparation, transfer the soup to a microwave-safe bowl with enough capacity to allow safe stirring. Cover the bowl loosely, heat it in intervals and stir between intervals to distribute the heat through the soup. Continue until the entire portion is thoroughly hot.

After heating, divide the soup evenly between two serving bowls. Handle the saucepan, microwave-safe bowl and heated soup carefully, because the container, soup and rising steam may all be hot. Allow the soup to stand briefly if necessary before serving.

Comprehensive guidance

How to prepare and serve GardenSoup S400

Follow these steps to inspect, open, heat, stir and serve the contents of one 400 gram can as two portions.

  1. 1

    Check and open the can

    Inspect the can before opening. Do not use the product if the can is swollen, leaking, heavily dented, rusted or otherwise damaged. Place the can on a stable surface and open it carefully with the fitted opening mechanism or a suitable can opener. Take care around the lid and open metal edge.

  2. 2

    Transfer the soup

    Pour all of the tomato and vegetable soup into a saucepan for stovetop preparation or into a microwave-safe bowl for microwave preparation. Use a spoon to remove any remaining vegetables or soup from the can. Do not heat the metal can directly and do not place it in a microwave oven.

  3. 3

    Heat and stir thoroughly

    For stovetop preparation, heat the soup over a medium setting and stir regularly. For microwave preparation, cover the bowl loosely, heat the soup in intervals and stir between intervals. Continue until the soup is thoroughly hot throughout, including at the centre of the container.

  4. 4

    Divide and serve

    Carefully divide the heated soup between two serving bowls. Distribute the vegetables and tomato base evenly between the portions. Allow the soup to stand briefly if necessary, check that it is at a suitable serving temperature and handle the hot bowls with care.

Important information

Preparation Safety and Storage

Follow the can-handling, heating and storage precautions below when preparing QuickHarvest GardenSoup S400.

Before heating

Check the can and open it carefully

  • Do not use a can that is swollen, leaking, heavily dented, rusted or otherwise damaged.
  • Place the can on a stable surface before opening it.
  • Use a suitable can opener if the can does not include an opening mechanism.
  • Keep fingers away from the lid and any sharp metal edge.
  • Transfer the complete contents out of the can before heating.
During heating

Use a suitable container and stir

  • Never place the metal can in a microwave oven.
  • Do not use the unopened or opened can as a stovetop heating container.
  • Use a suitable saucepan or a microwave-safe bowl.
  • Stir regularly or between microwave intervals for even heating.
  • Handle the hot container, soup and escaping steam carefully.
After serving

Store any unused soup separately

Serve GardenSoup S400 after it has been heated thoroughly and divided into two portions. If any soup is not served, transfer the unused portion to a clean, separate covered container and refrigerate it promptly. Do not keep leftover soup in the opened metal can.

When reheating refrigerated soup, use a suitable saucepan or microwave-safe bowl, stir during heating and make sure the soup is thoroughly hot before serving. Handle the reheated soup and container carefully.
