Important information

Safety and Storage

Handle the can, heated soup, hot container and unused portion carefully.

Before heating

Check the can

  • Do not use a swollen, leaking or heavily damaged can.
  • Open the can carefully on a stable surface.
  • Remove the complete contents before heating.
During heating

Use a suitable container

  • Never place the metal can in a microwave.
  • Use a saucepan or microwave-safe bowl.
  • Stir the soup and handle steam carefully.
After serving

Store unused soup separately

Transfer unused soup to a separate covered container and refrigerate it promptly.
