Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding Potato Chips Classic, including ingredients, recommended usage, storage conditions, quality testing, and product information.

Q What is Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic is a premium potato snack made from carefully selected Canadian organic potatoes. It is thinly sliced, lightly salted, and prepared to deliver a crisp and crunchy texture.
Q Who manufactures Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic is manufactured by Potato Foods International, a global snack food company specializing in the development, manufacturing, and distribution of high-quality snack products.
Q Who is Potato Chips Classic intended for?
A Potato Chips Classic is intended for consumers of all ages who enjoy a convenient ready-to-eat snack made from simple ingredients.
Q What are the key features of Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic is made from Canadian organic potatoes and features a light, crispy texture, lightly salted flavor, simple ingredients, and no artificial flavors.
Q What ingredients are used in Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic contains potatoes, sunflower vegetable oil, and salt. Per 100 grams, the product contains 65% potatoes, 33% sunflower oil, and 2% salt.
Q How should Potato Chips Classic be consumed?
A Potato Chips Classic is a ready-to-eat snack and can be enjoyed directly from the package without any preparation.
Q What type of packaging is used for Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic is available in Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene pillow bags and stand-up pouches designed to maintain product freshness and quality.
Q How long is the shelf life of Potato Chips Classic?
A Potato Chips Classic has a shelf life of three months when stored according to the recommended storage conditions.
Q How should Potato Chips Classic be stored?
A The product should be stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive humidity. Consumers should not consume the product if the packaging is damaged, opened before purchase, or appears inflated.
Q Has Potato Chips Classic undergone quality and safety testing?
A Yes. Potato Chips Classic undergoes quality-control testing, including reducing sugar testing, specific gravity testing, dry matter and moisture analysis, size and shape grading, color measurement, texture analysis, moisture and oil content testing, metal detection, X-ray inspection, and packaging integrity inspection. All reported tests were passed.
