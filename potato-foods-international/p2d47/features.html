Ingredients & Features

Potato Chips Classic is prepared from organic Canadian potatoes and lightly seasoned with salt.

Key Features

  • Made from Canadian organic potatoes
  • Light and crispy texture
  • Simple ingredients
  • Lightly salted flavor
  • No artificial flavors
  • Convenient ready-to-eat snack
  • Suitable for sharing
  • Consistent quality and freshness

Ingredient Composition

Ingredient Weight (g) Percentage

Product Development Careers

Food technologists, sensory coordinators, procurement specialists, packaging teams, and quality employees can contribute different skills to product-development and improvement projects.

Nutrition and Wellbeing Education

Voluntary employee sessions may explain general nutrition literacy, balanced eating, hydration, shift-work meal planning, and the distinction between public information and personal medical advice.

03

Supplier Responsibility Engagement

Corporate responsibility work may involve communicating expectations on ingredient documentation, workplace standards, traceability, transport practices, and responsible sourcing with suppliers.

04

Skills Recognition

Competency records and development reviews can recognise progress in technical knowledge, communication, documentation, teamwork, and operational problem-solving.
