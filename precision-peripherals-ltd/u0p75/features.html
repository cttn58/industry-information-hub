Components & Features

Wireless Mouse Y1 combines 2.4GHz wireless technology, Bluetooth connectivity, optical tracking, ergonomic comfort, and long battery life.

Zero-lag wireless connectivity, multi-device switching, silent clicks, precision tracking and up to 200 hours battery life.

ABS and polycarbonate housing, optical sensor, wireless module, circuit board, buttons, scroll wheel, AA battery, USB receiver and PTFE feet pads.

Product Claims

Reliable wireless productivity with high precision tracking and extended battery life.
