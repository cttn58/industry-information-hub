About Wireless Mouse Y1

Wireless Mouse Y1 is a high-performance wireless mouse designed to provide precision, comfort, and seamless connectivity for everyday computing and professional work.

The mouse supports switching between desktop computers, laptops, and tablets, with ergonomic design and high-precision optical tracking.

Battery life reaches up to 200 hours under normal operating conditions.

Product Information

Wireless Mouse Y1 provides 2.4GHz connectivity, Bluetooth multi-device support, high precision tracking, five-year recommended service life, and dry room-temperature storage guidance.
