Frequently Asked Questions

Information about Wireless Mouse Y1 connectivity, battery life, features, packaging, storage, and quality testing.

Wireless Mouse Y1 supports 2.4GHz and Bluetooth connectivity, multi-device switching, precision optical tracking and up to 200 hours of battery life.

The product is manufactured by Precision Peripherals Ltd and has a recommended service life of five years.

The package includes the mouse, USB receiver, guides, safety information, warranty card and protective packaging.

Quality-control tests cover components, pairing, tracking, controls, battery, durability, EMC and final inspection.
