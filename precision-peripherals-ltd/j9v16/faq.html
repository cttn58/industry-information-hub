Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding Wireless Mouse Y1, including wireless connectivity, multi-device Bluetooth support, battery life, product features, packaging, storage conditions, and quality-control testing.

Wireless Mouse Y1 is a high-performance wireless mouse designed to provide precision, comfort, and seamless connectivity for everyday computing and professional work. It features advanced 2.4GHz wireless technology and Bluetooth multi-device connectivity for responsive performance comparable to a wired mouse.

Wireless Mouse Y1 is manufactured by Precision Peripherals Ltd, a consumer electronics company specializing in the design, development, manufacturing, and distribution of computer peripherals and productivity accessories.

Wireless Mouse Y1 is intended for all users and is suitable for office work, creative applications, study, and general computing.

Key features include zero-lag 2.4GHz wireless connectivity with wired-like performance, Bluetooth multi-device connectivity, quick switching between paired devices, a high-precision optical tracking sensor, ergonomic right-handed design, silent click buttons, adjustable DPI settings, and up to 200 hours of battery life.

The mouse includes a 2.4GHz RF and Bluetooth wireless module, a high-precision optical sensor for cursor tracking, adjustable DPI functionality, multi-device switching capability, and a nano USB RF receiver for wireless connectivity.

Wireless Mouse Y1 is designed for everyday computing and professional work. It can be used with desktop computers, laptops, and tablets, and supports seamless switching between multiple connected devices for improved productivity.

The package includes the Wireless Mouse Y1, a USB receiver, quick-start guides, safety information, warranty cards, and protective packaging consisting of a heavy-duty outer box and a custom recycled paper pulp inner mold.

Wireless Mouse Y1 has a recommended service life of five years from the manufacturing date under normal usage and storage conditions.

Store Wireless Mouse Y1 at room temperature in a clean, dry environment. Keep relative humidity below 90% to help prevent corrosion of electronic components. Avoid direct sunlight, excessive heat, strong magnetic fields, and exposure to liquids. Remove the battery if the product will not be used for an extended period.

Wireless Mouse Y1 undergoes extensive quality-control testing, including electronic component verification, battery compartment inspection, PCB functionality testing, material and housing inspection, Bluetooth module verification, incoming quality control (IQC) inspection, wireless connectivity testing, Bluetooth pairing testing, optical tracking accuracy testing, button and scroll wheel function testing, battery performance testing, multi-device switching testing, drop and durability testing, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) testing, and final visual inspection. All reported tests were passed.
