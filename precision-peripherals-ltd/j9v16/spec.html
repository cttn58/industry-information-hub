Product Specifications

Physical Description

Wireless Mouse Y1 is an ergonomic wireless mouse designed for right-handed use with a matte black finish, silent buttons, integrated side buttons and a precision scroll wheel.

Packaging

Heavy-duty solid cardboard outer box with printed product specifications and features, custom recycled paper pulp inner mold, USB receiver, quick-start guides, safety information, a warranty cards.

Warnings

Prolonged exposure to moisture may damage electronic components, do not disassemble or modify the device, remove batteries before long-term storage to prevent leakage, and keep small accessories such as the USB receiver away from young children.
