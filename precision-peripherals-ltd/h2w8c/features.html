Components & Features

Wireless Mouse Y1 combines advanced 2.4GHz wireless technology, Bluetooth multi-device connectivity, precision optical tracking, ergonomic comfort, and up to 250 hours of battery life in a compact wireless design.

Key Features

Wireless Mouse Y1 includes zero-lag wireless connectivity, Bluetooth support, multi-device switching, automatic device recognition, ergonomic design, silent click buttons, high-precision tracking and battery life of up to 250 hours.

Component Composition

The product contains a mouse housing made from ABS and polycarbonate materials, a high precision optical sensor, wireless communication module, printed circuit board, primary buttons, scroll wheel, side buttons, AA battery, USB receiver and PTFE feet pads.

Product Claims

Wireless Mouse Y1 provides zero-lag 2.4GHz connectivity, Bluetooth multi-device connectivity, high precision optical tracking and extended battery life of up to 250 hours. The device supports USB RF receiver connectivity and is designed for reliable wireless productivity.
