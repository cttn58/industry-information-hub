Product Specifications

Physical Description

  • Ergonomic wireless mouse designed for right-handed use
  • Matte black finish with premium appearance
  • Textured grip surfaces for improved handling
  • Precision scroll wheel with tactile feedback
  • Silent-click button mechanism
  • Integrated side navigation buttons
  • Optical tracking sensor located on the base
  • USB receiver storage compartment included

Packaging

  • Heavy-duty solid cardboard outer box with printed product specifications and features
  • Custom recycled paper pulp inner mold
  • USB wireless receiver
  • Quick-start guide
  • Safety information document
  • Warranty card

Warnings

  • Prolonged exposure to moisture may damage electronic components
  • Do not disassemble or modify the device
  • Remove batteries before long-term storage to prevent leakage
  • Keep small accessories, including the USB receiver, away from young children
