Components & Features

Wireless Mouse Y1 combines advanced 2.4GHz wireless technology, Bluetooth multi-device connectivity, precision optical tracking, ergonomic comfort, and up to 200 hours of battery life in a compact wireless design.

Key Features

Connectivity Features

  • Zero-lag 2.4GHz connectivity
  • Bluetooth multi-device connectivity
  • Quick switching between devices

Performance Features

  • High precision optical tracking
  • Adjustable DPI settings

Comfort Features

  • Ergonomic design
  • Silent click buttons

Battery Features

  • Up to 200 hours battery life
