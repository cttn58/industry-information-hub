Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1, including vacuum insulation, temperature retention performance, product features, packaging, storage conditions, and quality-control testing.

Q What is Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
A Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is a premium double-wall vacuum-insulated bottle designed to keep beverages hot or cold for extended periods. Manufactured from food-grade 304 stainless steel, it provides durability, corrosion resistance, and safe beverage storage without affecting taste or odor.
Q Who manufactures Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
A Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is manufactured by AquaSteel Drinkware, a manufacturer and distributor of premium stainless steel drinkware products designed for everyday hydration, travel, sports, outdoor activities, and professional use.
Q Who is Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 intended for?
A Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is intended for all users and is suitable for everyday hydration, commuting, office use, travel, sports, camping, hiking, and other outdoor activities.
Q What are the key features of Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
A Key features include double-wall vacuum insulation technology, temperature retention for cold beverages for more than 24 hours and hot beverages for more than 12 hours, food-grade 304 stainless steel construction, BPA-free, BPS-free and phthalate-free materials, a leak-proof airtight lid, corrosion-resistant construction, and a reusable environmentally friendly design.
Q What technologies are included in Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
A The bottle includes double-wall vacuum insulation technology for temperature retention, food-grade 304 stainless steel construction, a food-grade polypropylene lid assembly, a food-grade silicone sealing ring, and a powder-coated exterior finish for protection and appearance.
Q How should Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 be used?
A Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is designed for daily hydration and is suitable for both hot and cold beverages. It can be used for commuting, office use, travel, sports, camping, hiking, and other outdoor activities.
Q What is included in the package?
A The package includes Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1, an unbleached brown kraft paper box with minimal color printing, and a thin poly bag that helps prevent scratches to the bottle exterior finish during transportation and storage.
Q How long is the shelf life of Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
A Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 has a recommended service life of 12 years from the date of manufacture under normal usage and storage conditions.
Q How should Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 be stored?
A Store the bottle clean and completely dry when not in use. Leave the lid slightly open during long-term storage to promote air circulation. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive humidity.
Q Has Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 undergone quality and safety testing?
A Yes. Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 undergoes stainless steel grade verification, material composition analysis, food-contact safety verification, silicone seal inspection, vacuum-layer integrity inspection, coating inspection, insulation performance testing, retention testing, leak-proof testing, drop resistance, corrosion resistance, coating adhesion, and final visual inspection. All reported tests were passed.
