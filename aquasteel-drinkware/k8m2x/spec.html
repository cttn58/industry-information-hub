Product Specifications

Physical Description

  • Stainless steel vacuum-insulated water bottle
  • Double-wall construction
  • Smooth powder-coated exterior finish
  • Cylindrical design with ergonomic grip
  • Leak-resistant screw-top lid
  • Food-grade contact surfaces
  • Scratch-resistant exterior coating
  • Clean and odor-free interior surface

Packaging

  • An unbleached brown kraft paper box with minimal color printing
  • A thin poly bag prevents the bottle's paint from scratches

Warnings

  • Do not use if the bottle or lid is damaged
  • Keep hot beverages out of reach of young children to prevent burns
  • Do not place the bottle in a microwave oven
  • Do not use with carbonated beverages unless specifically designed for such use
  • Ensure the lid is securely tightened before transport
  • Clean thoroughly before first use and after each use

MTH

Modular Arithmetic

Modular arithmetic examines remainders produced when numbers are divided by a selected value. The system is often described using repeating numerical cycles and is applied in areas such as scheduling, coding theory, and mathematical problem solving.

HIS

Ancient Coin Minting

Early coins were produced by preparing metal blanks and striking the surfaces with engraved dies. Images, symbols, inscriptions, and weight standards helped communicate authority, regional identity, and systems of exchange in historical societies.

TRN

Mountain Cable Railways

Cable railway systems transport vehicles along steep routes using coordinated cables, tracks, braking systems, and station controls. Their design responds to elevation changes, terrain, passenger movement, and the practical challenges of operating in mountainous regions.

BIO

Seed Dispersal Methods

Plants distribute seeds through wind, animals, flowing water, gravity, and mechanical release. Seed shape, surface texture, weight, and surrounding environmental conditions influence how far seeds travel from the original plant.
