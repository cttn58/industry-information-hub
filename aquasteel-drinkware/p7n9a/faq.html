QWho manufactures Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?

ASteel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is intended for all users and is suitable for everyday hydration, commuting, office use, travel, sports, camping, hiking, and other outdoor activities.
QWhat are the key features of Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
AKey features include double-wall vacuum insulation technology, temperature retention for cold beverages for more than 24 hours and hot beverages for more than 12 hours, food-grade 304 stainless steel construction, BPA-free, BPS-free and phthalate-free materials, a leak-proof airtight lid, corrosion-resistant construction, and a reusable environmentally friendly design.
QWhat technologies are included in Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
AThe bottle includes double-wall vacuum insulation technology for temperature retention, food-grade 304 stainless steel construction, a food-grade polypropylene lid assembly, a food-grade silicone sealing ring, and a powder-coated exterior finish for protection and appearance.
QHow should Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 be used?
ASteel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 is designed for daily hydration and is suitable for both hot and cold beverages. It can be used for commuting, office use, travel, sports, camping, hiking, and other outdoor activities.

QHow long is the shelf life of Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1?
ASteel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 has a recommended service life of 12 years from the date of manufacture under normal usage and storage conditions.
QHow should Steel Vacuum Water Bottle A1 be stored?
AStore the bottle clean and completely dry when not in use. Leave the lid slightly open during long-term storage to promote air circulation. Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and excessive humidity.
